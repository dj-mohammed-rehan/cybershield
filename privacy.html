Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the CyberShield website handles, and what it does not. CyberShield is a cyber safety and digital fraud awareness project. The short version: this website does not collect your personal information.

1. What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics.
  • No contact forms, so no names, email addresses, or phone numbers are submitted.
  • The quiz runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and score are not sent to any server.

2. Technical logs

Like almost every website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may record standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

3. Third-party links

This site links to official government services such as cybercrime.gov.in and sancharsaathi.gov.in. When you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply, and we encourage you to read them. We are not responsible for the content or practices of external sites.

4. Children

This site is educational and contains no age-restricted content. Because the site collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children either.

5. Changes to this policy

If the CyberShield app launches features that collect information (for example, an account or a fraud-reporting form), this policy will be updated before those features go live, and the date at the top will change.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nasaspaceappsastrocare@gmail.com.
